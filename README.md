# PSX Skyboxes II Free

**4 free PS1-style skies for your retro 3D game**: a clear sunrise, a green aurora, a red hell
sky and falling snow. Each is 32 colours, ordered-dithered like the PS1's textures and low-res on
purpose, as a seamless panorama and a cubemap, with ready sky materials and environments for
Godot. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Skyboxes II](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-skyboxes-2)** has 27: three each of sunrise,
sunset, aurora, eclipse (a total one, a ring of fire, a blood moon), hell (fire, a burning rift),
alien (two moons, a gas giant, a portal), smoke, underwater and snow, and a Unity package. Same
addon: it installs over this one. The everyday skies, night, dusk, day, storm, fog and space, are
in [PSX Skyboxes](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-skyboxes), with
[four free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-skyboxes-free) too.

### What's inside

- **Sunrise:** clear, with the pink belt of Venus opposite the sun.
- **Aurora:** green curtains under the stars.
- **Hell:** a red sky under burning cloud.
- **Snow:** falling flakes under a pale overcast.
- **Every sky as a panorama** (1024 x 512, and 512 x 256) **and a cubemap** (256 px faces, and
  128), with no seam, 32 colours each.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with, for each sky, an Environment (the sky, ambient light and reflections from it, and
fog of its horizon's colour): put it on a WorldEnvironment and you're done. The skies alone are
materials too, a panorama one and a cubemap one, and the viewer shows them over a plain. Tested on
Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns your level over in
one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

### Any engine

The panoramas (equirectangular, the middle column ahead) and the cube faces in OpenGL's order
and orientation. Set point filtering and turn mipmaps off.
